# Gegërisht / Gegnisht — Gheg Albanian

Gheg is a dialect group with regional and literary traditions, not one uniformly
spelled dialect. Start with the user's voice sample, locality, and period.
Without them, use readable, restrained contemporary Gheg and avoid claiming an
exact Shkodër, Tirana, Dibra, or other local voice. For a Kosovo audience, read
[the Kosovo profile](sq-kosovo.md) when that regional choice is intended.

## Choices with grammatical consequences

- A `me` + participle construction is characteristic and often natural:
  `me punu`, `me ardhë`, `me shkue`. Its exact participle form varies by locality
  and written convention. It is not a replacement rule for every `të`.
- Short or other regionally established participles may suit the sample:
  `kam punu` or `kam punue`. Keep one coherent convention instead of alternating
  `punu/punue/punuar` randomly in the same voice.
- `asht` / `âsht` can fit particular literary or northern written conventions.
  `osht` fits some colloquial voices. Do not distribute them at random or apply
  any one of them to all Gheg speakers.
- Forms such as `nji`, `ma`, or a sample's `tash` can be appropriate. Choose them
  as part of actual sentences, not a quota of visible dialect words.
- Preserve dialectal forms where meaning and consistency are clear. Do not mark
  a native Gheg form as a learner mistake or “fix” it to standard by default.

Do not infer vowel length or nasalization from standard spelling and add accents
mechanically. Use diacritics such as `â` only when the chosen convention warrants
them. For `dialect_strength=strong`, seek a locality/sample if it is missing;
strong does not mean exaggerated spelling or more slang.

## Illustrative options, not universal equivalents

Standard: `Dua të flas me ty për këtë punë.`

Broad Gheg example: `Due me folë me ty për këtë punë.`

Standard: `E kam lexuar letrën, por nuk kam vendosur ende.`

One written Gheg rendering: `E kam lexue letrën, por ende s'kam vendosë.`

These illustrate a convention. A supplied `du/lexu` voice should not be forced
into `due/lexue`. Keep person, intent, tense, and uncertainty unchanged.

Make formal Gheg clear and dignified when requested. Do not silently convert it
to standard merely because the subject is serious. Conversely, a request for
standard Albanian should standardize the prose even if its author is Gheg-speaking.
