# Editing for a real reader

These are diagnostic questions, not evidence of authorship or a blacklist.
Preserve an intentional choice that suits the writer, reader, or genre.

## Paragraphs before vocabulary

Find the point of the passage. Remove introductions that merely announce it,
closers that repeat it, and background already known to the intended reader.
Keep necessary context for a standalone document. Check whether several sections
repeat the same opening–three examples–lesson template. Reorganize only where
that repetition gets in the way.

| Pattern to inspect | Albanian / English signal | Better decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Empty importance | “një hap historik drejt së ardhmes”; “a transformative milestone” | State the actual change; keep justified significance |
| Theatrical contrast | “Nuk është thjesht X, por Y”; “not merely X, but Y” | Keep both sides if they make a real distinction; otherwise state the point |
| Announced explanation | “Le të zhytemi në…”; “Let's dive into…” | Begin with the information |
| Vague grandeur | “një mozaik mundësish”; “a tapestry of possibilities” | Use the subject's actual features |
| Unnamed authority | “Ekspertët thonë…”; “Experts agree…” | Preserve attribution; flag missing support instead of inventing experts |
| Inflated verbs | “bën të mundur realizimin e”; “facilitates the implementation of” | Usually a direct verb; preserve technical distinctions |
| Redundant hedging | “mund të jetë ndoshta…”; “could potentially possibly…” | Retain the actual degree of uncertainty |
| Mechanical rhythm | Every sentence begins “Për më tepër” / “Furthermore” | Let relations between ideas determine transitions |
| Decorative triplets | Three near-synonyms with no distinct content | Remove repetition, keeping genuinely separate requirements |
| Unneeded wrapper | “Ja teksti i përmirësuar”; “I hope this helps” inside the deliverable | Return the deliverable itself |
| Forced conclusion | Every section ends with a moral or a slogan | End where its useful content ends |
| Visual clutter | Every item has a bold label, emoji, and repeated description | Use the structure needed for reading and access |

Do not replace one stock phrase with a more casual stock phrase. “Here's the
thing” can be as formulaic as “It is important to note”. “In conclusion” can be
appropriate in a student essay. “Critical” may name a real severity category.

## Sentence decisions

Use the actor when known and useful; keep passive voice when the actor is unknown,
irrelevant, or intentionally withheld. Vary sentence length according to thought,
not a random pattern. A series of short sentences can sound artificial too.
Use a colon, comma, dash, or parenthesis when its grammatical job is useful.
Do not strip range dashes, dialogue punctuation, or the writer's preferred quotes.
Keep ordinary words such as “important”, “gjithashtu”, or “thelbësor” when earned.

In factual text, replace generalities only with details supplied by the user or
actually verified within an authorized research task. Never invent a concrete
detail to improve the sentence. In persuasive copy, make the benefit clear
without fabricating testimonials, scarcity, superiority, or performance.

## Original paired examples

Input: “Është e rëndësishme të theksohet se portali ynë inovativ ju mundëson që
ta realizoni dërgimin e kërkesës në internet. Ky është një hap i rëndësishëm.”

Edit: “Kërkesën mund ta dërgoni në internet përmes portalit tonë.”

Input: “It is worth noting that the office will potentially remain closed on
Monday if the repairs are not completed. This highlights our commitment to safety.”

Edit: “The office may remain closed on Monday if the repairs are not finished.
We are taking this precaution for safety.”

The second edit keeps the safety rationale because it is a claim in the source.
Do not drop a reason just because it arrived in a formulaic sentence.

Input: “You must submit an ID, proof of address, and a signed form.”

Edit: unchanged. Three required documents are not an ornamental triplet.

Input: “Nuk paguani çdo muaj; paguani vetëm kur e përdorni shërbimin.”

Edit: unchanged. The contrast explains the pricing model.
