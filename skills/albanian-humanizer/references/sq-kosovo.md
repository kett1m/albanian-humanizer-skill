# Shqipja e Kosovës — Kosovo Albanian

This profile concerns Albanian used in Kosovo. It is not a separate language or
a uniform dialect for every locality. Everyday varieties are largely Gheg;
standard Albanian is used in formal writing too. Route by register and audience,
not by an assumption that Kosovo writing must be slang.

## Formal and professional

For notices, reports, applications, academic writing, and formal business messages,
use standard grammar and spelling with appropriate Kosovo usage and the user's
terminology. Preserve institutional names, local service terms, dates, and money
as given. Do not convert lek to euros or rename institutions by inference.

Example: `Ju lutem, na e dërgoni dokumentin deri të premten.`

Do not insert `osht`, `qysh`, or `me bo` into that notice solely to signal Kosovo.
If the user explicitly asks for formal dialectal Gheg, honor that instead.

## Contemporary conversation

For personal messages, familiar social copy, or dialogue, a coherent light Kosovo
voice can use `me` constructions and short participles where natural:
`me punu`, `me folë`, `kam lexu`, `s'kam mujt`. Depending on the speaker,
`tash`, `qysh`, `çka`, `ma`, or `veç` may fit. They are options, not obligatory
replacements; they are not all exclusive to Kosovo.

Standard: `A mund të ma dërgosh dokumentin sot?`

Conversational example: `A mundesh me ma dërgu dokumentin sot?`

Standard: `Nuk mund të vij sot. A mund ta lëmë për nesër?`

Conversational example: `S'muj me ardhë sot. A mundemi me e lanë për nesër?`

Use `osht` only if the requested colloquial intensity or sample supports it;
standard `është` is not forbidden in a light regional voice. Avoid switching
between literary `âsht`, colloquial `osht`, and standard `është` without a reason.
Preserve meaningful code-switching from the writer. Do not add English words,
Turkish loans, profanity, `bre`, or `o vlla` to manufacture authenticity.

## Locale and spelling

Prishtina, Prizren, Peja, Gjakova, Gjilan, and rural communities do not all speak
the same way. Names are not a checklist of dialect traits. For a strongly local
voice, follow a sample or request a locality; do not guess a speaker's class,
education, or identity.

Default to readable spelling with `ë` and `ç` where the chosen convention uses
them. Preserve deliberate contractions consistently. Letter omission in texting
is an orthographic choice, not a requirement of Kosovo Albanian.
