# Sources and limits

Sources were consulted on 2026-11-16. Links support the stated principles; they
do not certify the original examples, the whole skill, or its model outputs.
No source text is required at runtime.

## Upstream writing projects

- [Siqi Chen / blader — Humanizer](https://github.com/blader/humanizer), inspected
  revision `b48c832f0f809c1b5658edbb838c2521b72f2413`: editorial attention to
  formulaic structure, factual fidelity, voice samples, and file-safe rewriting.
- [joeinvents — humanizerskill](https://github.com/joeinvents/humanizerskill),
  inspected revision `0b57fde610d0b44e2b2911c8bafd799bf1f76605`: conceptual
  inspiration to inspect narrative construction as well as surface phrasing.
- [Russell et al., StoryScope: Investigating idiosyncrasies in AI fiction](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.03136v6):
  investigates structural tendencies in sampled human and AI fiction. This skill
  does not generalize a detector accuracy figure to Albanian or require the
  inverse of every measured tendency as a writing rule.

See [third-party notices](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for licensing boundaries.
Blader credits Wikipedia's “Signs of AI writing”; this project does not bundle
Wikipedia text or copy its examples.

## Albanian

- [Riverin-Coutlée and colleagues, Dialect change and language attitudes in Albania](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/language-variation-and-change/article/dialect-change-and-language-attitudes-in-albania/630DB58685994F38CCEA6F4C400B7D05):
  supports distinguishing Gheg and Tosk, regional variation, and a largely
  Tosk-based standard without treating one dialect as linguistically inferior.
- [Victor A. Friedman, Language Politics and Language Policies in the Contemporary Western Balkans](https://www.wilsoncenter.org/publication/337-language-politics-and-language-policies-the-contemporary-western-balkans-infinitives):
  discusses the Gheg infinitive and its sociolinguistic setting, including Kosovo.
  Used for linguistic background, not as a current statement of government policy.
- [Akademia e Shkencave e Shqipërisë — Veprimtaria, gjuhësia](https://akad.gov.al/veprimtaria-gjuhesia/):
  identifies normative Albanian works including *Drejtshkrimi i gjuhës shqipe*
  (1973) and *Fjalori drejtshkrimor i gjuhës shqipe* (1976). For a disputed standard
  form, consult an appropriate dictionary or grammar, not this skill alone.

Dialect examples are deliberately limited editorial illustrations. This package
does not prescribe one official Gheg, Tosk, or Kosovo orthography. A speaker's
sample and locality are better evidence for a specific voice than a generic list.

## CEFR

- [Council of Europe — The CEFR Levels](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/level-descriptions):
  explains the six-level framework and its application across languages.
- [Council of Europe — Global scale, Table 1](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/table-1-cefr-3.3-common-reference-levels-global-scale):
  provides the proficiency descriptors informing the level labels.
- [CEFR Companion Volume (2020)](https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4):
  further reference for detailed communicative activities and assessment.

The level table in this package is an original editing heuristic, not a
reproduction of official descriptors or an endorsed assessment instrument.
