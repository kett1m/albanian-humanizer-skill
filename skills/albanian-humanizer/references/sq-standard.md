# Shqipja standarde — Standard Albanian

Use for general publishing, education, public information, professional prose,
or unspecified translation into Albanian. This is the codified shared standard,
not a claim that regional speech is defective or that Tosk and standard are
identical in every respect.

Keep standard forms such as `është`, `kam punuar`, `dua të shkoj`, and `për të
punuar` in their appropriate grammatical contexts. Do not replace `të`-clauses
with a Gheg `me` construction unless the user requests Gheg or a quoted voice.
Avoid bureaucratic padding while retaining legally or technically necessary terms.

Use `ju` for a clearly formal address, preserving singular/plural meaning where
it matters. Existing `ti` may be right for a familiar reader or product voice.
Check `ë`, `ç`, agreement, clitics, punctuation, and natural word order. Never
“correct” a proper name using a generalized spelling rule.

Example, professional message:

Input: “Në kuadër të përmirësimit të bashkëpunimit tonë, dëshiroj t'ju bëj me
dije se dokumenti në fjalë është dërguar nga ana ime.”

Edit: “Jua kam dërguar dokumentin për të përmirësuar bashkëpunimin tonë.”

If the opening is merely a conventional courtesy rather than a real purpose,
`Jua kam dërguar dokumentin` may suffice. Do not remove a substantive purpose
without considering the context.

Example, direct request: `Ju lutem, dërgojeni dokumentin deri të premten.`

When standardizing an informal source, retain its intent: `S'muj me ardhë sot`
can become `Nuk mund të vij sot`, not `Nuk dua të vij sot`.
