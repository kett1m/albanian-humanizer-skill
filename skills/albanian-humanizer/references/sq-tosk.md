# Toskërisht — Tosk Albanian

Tosk is the southern dialect group. Standard Albanian is largely Tosk-based, but
“Tosk” does not designate a single local voice identical to the standard. Korçë,
Gjirokastër, Labëria, and other areas have distinct usages. A broad profile should
not pretend to reproduce all of them at once.

Without a locality or sample, use a restrained, broadly southern conversational
rendering with full readable spelling. It may closely resemble standard Albanian.
Do not manufacture differences just to make the label visible. If exact regional
color is central to the request, ask for locality or a short authentic sample.

Keep appropriate Tosk/standard-compatible constructions such as `dua të shkoj`,
`kam punuar`, and `për të folur`. Do not add Gheg `me punu`, `asht`, or `osht` as
generic informal Albanian. Preserve genuine regional forms from a sample without
turning every sentence into a phonetic transcription.

Example, broad conversational register:

Input: `Dëshiroj të të informoj se do të mbërrij pak më vonë, pasi kam ende punë.`

Edit: `Do të vij pak më vonë, se kam ende punë.`

This is compatible with broad Tosk speech; it is not a diagnostic marker of one
southern town. `Dua të flas me ty për këtë punë` can be natural in both Tosk and
standard Albanian. Do not force a different sentence merely to fill a comparison.

Do not import Arbëresh or Arvanitika forms into a present-day southern Albanian
voice without an explicit request. Do not invent regional proverbs, habitual
forms of address, or personality traits. For a named local variety, prioritize
the supplied speaker's consistent syntax and vocabulary over a generic profile.
